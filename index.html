OUR MISSION

Small actions can create big changes.

EcoBite is a food-waste awareness platform that encourages individuals, restaurants, hostels and event organizers to reduce unnecessary food waste and responsibly share surplus food.

THE PROBLEM

Why should we care about food waste?

Food waste is not only about throwing away food. It also means wasting the water, energy, land and human effort used to produce that food.

🍽️

Wasted Food

Excess food from homes, restaurants and events often ends up in waste bins.

🌍

Environmental Impact

Food waste contributes to unnecessary resource consumption and environmental pollution.

💧

Wasted Resources

Producing food requires water, energy, land and transportation.

FOOD SAVING TIPS

Simple habits, better future.

01

Plan Your Meals

Prepare a shopping list before buying groceries to avoid unnecessary purchases.

02

Store Food Properly

Proper storage can help maintain food quality and reduce spoilage.

03

Reuse Leftovers Safely

Store suitable leftovers safely and use them before they spoil.

04

Avoid Over-Ordering

Order only the amount of food that you are reasonably able to consume.

05

Share Surplus Food

When appropriate, consider sharing safe surplus food through trusted organizations.

06

Learn Food Safety

Understand basic food-storage and food-safety practices before sharing food.
